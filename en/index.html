Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to build a standard website?

+

Standard websites are built in 1-2 weeks, while larger projects and online stores require 3-8 weeks. It all depends on the scope of the project, amount of content, functionality and how ready the materials are.

How much does a website cost, and is the price set in advance?

+

The price is always clearly defined at the start of the project. There are no hidden costs; any additional functionality is agreed upon in advance, before it's built.

Does the price include maintenance, support and hosting?

+

Yes — for the first three months, maintenance, support and hosting are completely free for all standard packages. After that it's €10/month or €100/year. Promotional packages do not include the free first three months.

What does maintenance and technical support include after launch?

+

Maintenance covers security checks, backups, minor content updates, performance monitoring and technical support. Monthly site analysis reports are also available, so you always know the state of your online presence.

What do I need to get started on my website?

+

All you need is a description of your business and your services or products, the goals you want the site to achieve, a logo (if you have one) and any existing materials (photos, videos, pricing...). If you don't have enough — we'll shape the content together.

Do you do complete SEO optimization for the website?

+

Yes, we do complete SEO optimization: structure, content, meta data, speed, device responsiveness and technical parameters — everything needed to lift your site above the competition and everything needed for a better technical foundation, visibility and understanding of the site by Google Search.

Is Google marketing mandatory alongside website development?

+

It's not mandatory, but it can always be added as an extra service. When you want your website to speak louder, Google marketing can be the trumpet that carries your message far.
